# Employee Business Expense Policy — tailored edition

This is `policy-a.docx` (the Workable sample template) with every bracketed
placeholder filled in with a concrete value, and with the rules the template leaves
silent made explicit. Structure and wording follow the original; only the numbers
are added. Reporting currency: **THB**.

Policy A vs Policy B is the experiment's second axis: A measures whether a reviewer
invents rules that do not exist, B measures whether it applies rules that do.

## Policy brief & purpose

We reimburse employees for reasonable, approved, work-related expenses.

## Travel expenses — reimbursable

Accommodation; legal document expenses (e.g. visa); air, train, ship or other
transportation fares; necessary medical expenses (e.g. vaccinations); local
transportation during trips (taxi fares, rental cars); and meals, subject to the
caps below.

## Caps (per person, per occurrence)

- Breakfast: up to **250**
- Lunch: up to **500**
- Dinner: up to **800**
- Client or partner entertainment dinner: up to **1,200 per attendee**
- Accommodation: up to **3,500 per night**

Amounts above a cap are not claimable. Claim up to the cap and record the excess as
non-claimable, showing the arithmetic.

## Approval thresholds

- Any single expense above **3,000** requires manager approval before reimbursement.
  Route to the exception queue with reason `NEEDS-APPROVAL`.
- Entertainment expenses require manager approval regardless of amount.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

- Expenses incurred by spouses or other non-employees accompanying an employee.
  On a shared receipt, claim only the employee's own portion.
- Un-authorized service upgrades (e.g. business class, hotel room upgrades). Air
  tickets are booked in economy unless an upgrade is authorized in writing.
- Personal services (massages, beauty treatments).
- Personal purchases (gifts, clothes).
- Lost personal property (e.g. luggage).
- Fines incurred while driving a company or rental vehicle.
- Non-business subscriptions or training, and personal trips.
- **Alcohol** is not reimbursable at any amount. On a shared receipt, claim only
  the non-alcohol portion.

## Receipts, deadlines and duplicates

- Every claimed expense needs a legible receipt showing a date and a total. Missing
  either → exception `MISSING-DATA`. Never estimate an amount.
- Claims must be submitted within **90 days** of the trip end date. Later →
  exception `OUT-OF-WINDOW`.
- Receipts for business dinners and transportation must be submitted within
  **30 days**. Later → exception `OUT-OF-WINDOW`.
- An expense matching an existing row in the prior-claims ledger on vendor, date and
  amount is a duplicate → exception `DUPLICATE`.

## Currency

- The reporting currency is **THB**. A receipt in another currency is converted at
  the closing mid-market rate on the transaction date, and both the original and the
  converted amount are recorded.
